#### Notes on this project and dataset
The dataset only specifies that this data originates from 2 days in September 2013. This tells us nothing of the starting timestamp in relation to the time of day, and it's difficult to speculate on the day of the week and whether the data has multiple charges on the same credit card (no information is given, except for the 28 different features that have their principal values).

We are going to assume for now that the time starts at 2013-09-01 00:00:00
